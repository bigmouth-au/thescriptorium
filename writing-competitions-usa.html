US Writing Competitions & Literary Festivals in 2026

The United States has the world's largest and most varied literary competition ecosystem, with thousands of prizes across every genre and form — from the Pulitzer Prize to genre-specific screenplay contests like the PAGE Awards and Final Draft Big Break.

The US also hosts essential events for serious writers: the AWP Conference (the largest literary conference in North America), the Tin House Summer Workshop, the Bread Loaf Writers' Conference (America's oldest, running since 1926), and the Austin Film Festival's Screenwriters Conference.

Most US competitions are open internationally. Use the filters to find competitions and conferences by type — fiction, poetry, novel, flash, or screenplay.
